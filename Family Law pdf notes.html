MARRIAGE

Definition

Marriage is a voluntary union of a man and a woman whether monogamous or polygamous provided it is registerd as required under the marriage act.

Circumstances under which a marriage can be converted

  1. Change of faith

    2. If a party in a marrage transforms from upholding their customs and become a Christian that marriage will have been changed from being potentially polygamous to monogamous

  2. change in domicile

    3. When a couple moves to a jurisdiction whose laws allow them to conduct polytgamy thnen the marriage can potentially change from being mino to poly

  3. Conducting a subsequent ceremony

    4. When a couple has agreed to conduct a wedding ceremony after payment of dowery that marriage changes from poly to mono

  4. By statute

    5. If a statute is put in place that aalows for monogamy
